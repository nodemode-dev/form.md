# Demo credits: publish

Every picture the publish demo shows. The files in `assets/media/` are byte-identical copies of the pictures already credited for demo 1 in [`../../demo/CREDITS.md`](../../demo/CREDITS.md); `assets/media/pictures.js` holds the same fifteen images as base64 so the page can read them for export and for the published preview over file://. Source pages were opened again for this demo (2026-11-07) and the licence read on each. Nothing is AI generated.

## NASA images

Source: the NASA Image and Video Library record for each item, read as JSON at https://images-api.nasa.gov/search?nasa_id=ID and shown at https://images.nasa.gov/details/ID. The records carry no licence text, so the licence is taken from NASA's media guidelines, https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/, which says NASA content "generally are not subject to copyright in the United States" and that the NASA insignia and logotype "are not in the public domain".
